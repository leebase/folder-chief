# Memo: The "Folder Chief" Pre-Sales Wedge Strategy

**Context:** We use Folder Chief not just to organize our own work, but as a zero-friction gift we give to prospects to win deals.

## How it works:
Instead of sending a generic "thank you" email after a discovery call, we give the prospect a customized AI Chief of Staff. We say: 
*"I noticed you are struggling with [Pain Point]. I built this Folder Chief specifically for your role to help you manage that. Just drop it into your Claude or ChatGPT subscription."*

## Your Role as My Assistant:
When I dump raw notes from a discovery call into my journal, I want you to evaluate if this prospect is a good candidate for the Wedge Strategy. 

If they are struggling with:
- **Scattered architecture decisions:** Suggest I give them the *IT/Engineering Director* load.
- **Lost requirements and scope creep:** Suggest I give them the *Product Owner* load.
- **Reporting chaos and vendor sprawl:** Suggest I give them the *Data & Analytics Leader* load.
- **Status reporting fatigue:** Suggest I give them the *PMO* load.

When we decide to give a prospect a Chief, I will ask you to draft the "door note" (the email handing it over) tailored to the exact pain points we uncovered.
