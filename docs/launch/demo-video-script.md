# Folder Chief — 90-Second Demo Video Script

**Target Duration:** 90 seconds (1:30)  
**Format:** Screen recording (Terminal + Obsidian) with voiceover and background ambient sound.  
**Tagline:** *Yesterday it met me. Today it briefed me. It's a folder.*

---

### [0:00 – 0:15] Scene 1: The Hook & Quickstart

**Visual:**
* Clean, minimalist dark-mode terminal window on a desktop.
* Host typing in the terminal.

**Terminal Command:**
```bash
git clone https://github.com/leebase/folder-chief.git folder-chief
cd folder-chief
claude
```

**Voiceover (Calm, direct, confident):**
> "You already pay for the most capable AI on earth. It lives in your terminal. But every morning, it wakes up with total amnesia.
> 
> What if your AI Chief of Staff was just a folder?"

---

### [0:15 – 0:40] Scene 2: Session 1 — The First Meeting

**Visual:**
* Claude Code launches. Folder Chief introduces itself with three simple sentences.
* Prompt appears asking 3 questions: Name, work overview, what's on your plate right now.
* User types:
  `"I'm Lee. I run a consulting practice. Right now I'm prepping the Henderson proposal, but worried about their tight Q4 timeline and our team bandwidth."`
* Chief immediately creates `brain/me.md`, drafts a structured delivery timeline with risk analysis, and files a project note in `brain/notes/projects/henderson-proposal.md`.

**Voiceover:**
> "No setup wizard. In under three minutes, your Chief learns your active priorities, handles a real task, and files what it learned into human-readable Markdown notes that you own."

**Visual Action:**
* User closes the terminal window (`exit`).

---

### [0:40 – 0:50] Scene 3: Transition — The Next Morning

**Visual:**
* Fast-forward graphic or subtle time-lapse transition showing clock moving to 8:30 AM next day.
* Fresh terminal window opens.

**Terminal Command:**
```bash
cd folder-chief
claude
```

**User Input:**
`"Good morning. What should I focus on today?"`

**Voiceover:**
> "Next morning. Fresh session. Zero amnesia."

---

### [0:50 – 1:15] Scene 4: Session 2 — The Cross-Session Briefing

**Visual:**
* Chief responds instantly with structured, clear briefing:
  > *"Good morning Lee. Yesterday we mapped the Henderson proposal timeline risks. Here is where the deliverable stands, what changed overnight, and the one client decision that needs your approval before lunch."*
* Chief references `brain/notes/projects/henderson-proposal.md` and `brain/state/today.md`.

**Voiceover:**
> "It remembers your commitments, tracks your open loops, and separates what needs your decision from what's already handled.
> 
> No cloud server. No database. No background processes listening on open ports. Just plain files on your disk."

---

### [1:15 – 1:25] Scene 5: The Obsidian Graph Reveal

**Visual:**
* Smooth screen transition to Obsidian opening the `brain/` directory.
* The Obsidian Graph View animates, showing interconnected nodes: `me`, `henderson-proposal`, `commitments`, `tasks`.
* Click on `dashboards/tasks.base` showing clean task cards with status and due dates.

**Voiceover:**
> "Open the folder in Obsidian, and your entire business compiles into an interactive, visual knowledge graph and live task dashboards."

---

### [1:25 – 1:30] Scene 6: Closing & Call to Action

**Visual:**
* Return to terminal showing the 5-claim summary.
* Center text overlay:
  **Folder Chief**  
  *Your AI chief of staff is a folder.*  
  `github.com/leebase/folder-chief`

**Voiceover:**
> "Remembers you. Owned by you. Grows with you.
> 
> Clone it today on GitHub. Free and open source."

---

### Production Notes
* **Audio Track:** Subtle, modern low-fi electronic soundtrack.
* **Terminal Font:** JetBrains Mono or SF Mono, high contrast, 18pt font for crisp mobile viewing.
* **Obsidian Theme:** Default dark theme or Minimal theme with accent colors matching the terminal prompt.
