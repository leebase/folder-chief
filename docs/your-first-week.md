# Your First Week with Folder Chief

> **From first meeting to daily habit in five short sessions.**

Folder Chief is designed to deliver immediate value in session one, and undeniable continuity in session two. Here is a guided roadmap for your first five days.

---

## Day 1: Meet Your Chief
*Goal: Complete onboarding and solve one real problem.*

1. Open your terminal in `folder-chief` and launch your AI CLI (`claude`, `codex`, `gemini`, or `opencode`).
2. Answer the three introductory questions:
   - Your name
   - What your work looks like
   - Your top priority today
3. Give your Chief a real task right away: draft an outline, review a document, structure a project plan, or brainstorm solutions.
4. **Inspect the result:** Open `brain/me.md` and `brain/state/today.md` to see how your Chief captured your profile and priorities.

---

## Day 2: The Return Visit
*Goal: Experience the "session-2 recall" moment.*

1. The next morning, open your terminal in the folder and launch your AI CLI.
2. Say: *"Good morning. What should I focus on today?"*
3. **Watch what happens:** Your Chief re-enters from `brain/me.md`, the journal, and `brain/state/today.md`. If the daily brief is stale, it asks you to roll over or archive items rather than silently treating yesterday's priorities as current.
4. Confirm the rollover, update today's priorities, and tackle the next item on your list.

---

## Day 3: Add Projects & Context
*Goal: Ground your Chief in your real-world workload.*

1. Share the 3 to 5 major projects or responsibilities currently on your plate.
2. Drop background notes, briefing summaries, or project overviews into `brain/inbox/`.
3. Ask your Chief: *"Process my inbox notes and summarize our active project landscape."*
4. **Inspect the result:** Your Chief leaves raw source files untouched in `brain/sources/`, creates structured project notes in `brain/notes/projects/`, and updates `brain/index.md`.

---

## Day 4: Build Trust Through Correction
*Goal: Test memory authority and see how your Chief handles corrections.*

1. Open `brain/me.md` or one of your project notes in a text editor.
2. In your conversation, make an intentional correction:
   > *"Update my working style: I prefer bulleted summaries over narrative paragraphs, and our Q3 review is scheduled for September 15th, not September 1st."*
3. **Verify the behavior:** Your Chief updates the active files, records the knowledge change in `brain/log.md`, dates the superseded text, and records the behavioral lesson in `chief/learned/` as `observed-once` when applicable.
4. Ask a follow-up question about the Q3 review to confirm it immediately uses the corrected date.

---

## Day 5: Grow & Establish Rituals
*Goal: Discover recurring workflows and establish your first weekly rhythm.*

1. Ask your Chief: *"What recurring tasks or patterns have we handled this week?"*
2. Your Chief reviews your logs and identifies repetitive workflows (e.g., proposal drafting, meeting prep, weekly reporting).
3. **Choose your next step:**
   - **First Weekly Review:** Run your first interactive weekly planning session (see [Your First Automation](your-first-automation.md)).
   - **First Specialist:** If a responsibility has a distinct recurring focus, let your Chief help you scaffold a specialist workspace (see [Creating Your First Specialist](creating-your-first-agent.md)).

---

## Daily Habit Checklist

- **Morning (2 minutes):** Open terminal $\rightarrow$ launch CLI $\rightarrow$ *"What's on today's agenda?"*
- **During the Day:** Draft notes, analyze problems, and prepare communications inside the folder.
- **End of Day (1 minute):** Say *"wrap"* or *"done for today"*. Inspect the updated `journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md` and `brain/state/today.md` named in the Chief's one-line confirmation.
